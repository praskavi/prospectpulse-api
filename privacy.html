Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 June 2026

This draft explains how ProspectPulse handles account, billing, search, lead, and support information.

This page is a production-readiness template, not legal advice. Have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer before public production launch.

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as email address, user ID, profile metadata, plan, and credit balance.
  • Billing information handled by Stripe, including customer ID, subscription status, invoices, and payment events.
  • Search inputs such as category, location, state, postcodes, target roles, and enrichment settings.
  • Search outputs such as business names, websites, phone numbers, ratings, public listing data, and enriched contact details when available.
  • Usage records such as credit transactions, unlock events, search status, and webhook processing logs.
  • Support information you provide when contacting us.

How We Use Information

  • To provide search, enrichment, saved results, credit history, and account features.
  • To process subscriptions, plan changes, cancellations, and invoices through Stripe.
  • To enforce plan limits, credit usage, trial eligibility, and cancellation lockout rules.
  • To troubleshoot failed searches, billing issues, webhook processing, and customer support requests.
  • To improve product reliability, fraud prevention, and operational monitoring.

Lead And Contact Data

ProspectPulse may process business and contact data from public or third-party sources. Availability and accuracy can vary. Customers are responsible for using exported or copied lead data lawfully, including complying with privacy, spam, marketing, and consent requirements that apply to their outreach.

Service Providers

We use service providers to operate the product, including Supabase for authentication/database services, Stripe for billing, Redis/BullMQ for job processing, n8n and data-source workflows for automation, and hosting/logging providers. These providers process data only as needed to deliver the service.

Retention

Search result retention depends on plan configuration. Current product targets are Free: 7 days, Starter: 30 days, and Growth: 90 days. Billing, credit transaction, audit, and security records may be retained longer where needed for accounting, dispute resolution, abuse prevention, and legal compliance.

Your Choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of account information by contacting support. Some records may need to be retained for billing, security, legal, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@prospectpulse.com.au. Replace this address before production if a different support inbox is used.
