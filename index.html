Data Model

Built around user searches, not bulk lead imports

Each run creates a search record, then stores matching businesses in search results. This keeps the MVP simple, user-scoped, and ready for cleanup after 30 days.

  • Searches track category, location, status, and result counts.
  • Search results store business details, email, LinkedIn, phone, rating, and raw Apify payload.
  • Contact enrichment is explicit so users understand cost before running a scan.

Features

Everything you need to find and close local business leads

One workflow from search to enriched contact — no spreadsheets, no manual lookup.

🗺️

Google Places Discovery

Search any business category in any city or postcode. Get name, address, phone, website, and rating instantly.

🔗

LinkedIn Enrichment

Find decision-makers linked to each business — owners, directors, and managers with public LinkedIn profiles.

✉️

Email Verification

Verify discovered emails in real time before they hit your outbox. Only deliverable addresses, no bounces.

📋

Search History

Every scan is saved to your account. Re-open any past search and filter, sort, or export results anytime.

⚡

Credit-Based Usage

Credits are deducted from returned output and unlock actions. Failed searches should not reduce your balance.

🛡️

Per-User Data Isolation

Your leads are yours. Every search is scoped to your account with Supabase RLS enforcing access at the DB level.
