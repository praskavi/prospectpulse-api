Data Handling

Last updated: 17 June 2026

This page explains how ProspectPulse stores and protects customer search data and lead results.

Search Records

Each search is stored with the user ID, category, location, filters, status, estimated credits, actual credits, result counts, creation date, and expiry date.

Search Results

Results may include public business information, websites, phone numbers, ratings, public listing URLs, enriched owner/contact fields, LinkedIn URLs, emails where available, and raw provider payloads used for troubleshooting.

Access Controls

  • Customer-facing search results are scoped to the logged-in user.
  • Admin access is restricted to users in the `admin_users` allowlist.
  • Service-role keys and Stripe secrets stay on the backend.
  • Admin pages are internal and marked `noindex`.

Retention

Current retention targets are Free: 7 days, Starter: 30 days, and Growth: 90 days. Some operational records, such as billing events, credit transactions, webhook logs, and admin audit records, may be retained longer.

Exports

When customers export or copy results, they become responsible for storing, securing, updating, and using that data lawfully outside ProspectPulse.

Operational Monitoring

ProspectPulse keeps internal records for Stripe webhooks, credit transactions, failed searches, and queue status to support billing accuracy and incident response.
