Support

Contact ProspectPulse

Send billing, credit, account, privacy, or search support questions to the ProspectPulse team.

Billing or refunds

Include your account email, Stripe invoice ID, and a short description.

Failed search or credits

Include your account email, search ID if available, expected credit balance, and what happened.

Privacy or data requests

Include your account email and whether you are requesting access, correction, deletion, or another privacy action.

Security concern

Include a clear description, affected page/API if known, and steps to reproduce. Do not include secrets in the form.

Before Contacting Support

  • Check the FAQ for credit, search, and billing behaviour.
  • For invoices and payment methods, open Billing & Invoices from the app menu.
  • For failed searches, try a smaller location or simpler category before resubmitting.
