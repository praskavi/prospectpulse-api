💰 Apify Credit Cost Model
🗺️
Base: Place Scraped
$0.004 per place + $0.007 Actor start. 1,000 places = ~$4.00 base cost. Your margin lives here.
Apify pay-per-event pricing — cost scales with usage, not subscriptions
📇
Contact Details Add-on
+$0.002 per place for emails, social profiles. Only charge if business has a website. Charge users 5 credits to unlock — premium margin.
Unit economics: what Apify charges you vs what you charge the user
🔍
Filter Add-ons
+$0.001 per filter per place. Category filter, min star rating, website availability. Multiple categories count as one event.
How filter costs compound — design search defaults carefully
💳
Credit Economy (your pricing)
1 business credit = 1 lead record. 5 contact credits = unlocked contact. This markup funds your margin above Apify costs.
SaaS credit-based monetisation — decouple cost from consumption
