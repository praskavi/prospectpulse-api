Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 June 2026

This draft explains how refund and credit adjustment requests are handled.

Template only. Final refund rules should be reviewed before production and aligned with Stripe settings.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions renew until cancelled. Cancelling stops future renewal according to the Stripe subscription state. We generally do not provide automatic refunds for unused time unless required by law or approved after review.

Failed Searches

If a search fails before usable results are saved, credits should not be deducted. If credits were incorrectly deducted for a failed search, contact support with your account email and search ID so we can investigate and adjust credits where appropriate.

Duplicate Or Accidental Charges

If you believe you were charged twice, upgraded the wrong account, or experienced a billing error, contact support within 14 days with the Stripe invoice or payment reference. Approved refunds are processed through Stripe.

Data Quality

Lead data availability and accuracy can vary. Refunds are not normally provided solely because a lead does not respond, an email bounces, a contact is unavailable, or a business no longer matches your target profile.

How To Request Help

Email support@prospectpulse.com.au with your account email, invoice ID, search ID if relevant, and a short description of the issue.
