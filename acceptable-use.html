Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 17 June 2026

ProspectPulse is for lawful business lead research and responsible outreach.

Allowed Use

  • Finding and organising business prospects.
  • Researching public business information.
  • Enriching contacts where permitted by your plan.
  • Exporting results for lawful sales, marketing, partnerships, or customer research.

Prohibited Use

  • Spam, harassment, deceptive outreach, phishing, or impersonation.
  • Collecting sensitive personal information or targeting vulnerable people.
  • Bypassing authentication, credits, plan limits, rate limits, or security controls.
  • Reselling data in violation of law, third-party rights, or provider terms.
  • Using the service to discriminate unlawfully or build harmful profiling systems.

Outreach Responsibility

You are responsible for ensuring your outreach complies with applicable spam, privacy, marketing, and consent laws. Include required sender identification, unsubscribe mechanisms, and lawful basis or consent where required.

Enforcement

We may suspend or restrict accounts, withhold credits, remove access to exports, or terminate access where we suspect misuse, abuse, fraud, or legal risk.
