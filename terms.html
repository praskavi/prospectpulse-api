Using ProspectPulse

You must use ProspectPulse for lawful business prospecting and research only. You are responsible for your account, login credentials, search inputs, exported data, and outreach activity.

Credits And Plans

  • Credits are used for searches, returned results, contact unlocks, and enrichment features according to the active plan.
  • Credits are generally deducted based on output returned or actions completed, not merely the maximum input selected.
  • Failed searches should not deduct credits where no usable result is saved.
  • Plan limits, credit allocations, retention periods, and feature availability may change with notice.

Subscriptions And Billing

Paid subscriptions are processed by Stripe. By purchasing a paid plan, you authorise recurring billing until you cancel. Plan upgrades, downgrades, cancellations, invoices, payment methods, and billing portal access are handled through Stripe and ProspectPulse account workflows.

Data Accuracy

Lead and contact data can be incomplete, stale, duplicated, or inaccurate. ProspectPulse does not guarantee lead quality, deliverability, response rates, conversion, or revenue. You should verify important information before relying on it.

Customer Responsibilities

  • Use leads, exports, and contact details in compliance with privacy, spam, marketing, and consumer protection laws.
  • Do not use ProspectPulse for harassment, scraping abuse, credential collection, unlawful surveillance, or spam.
  • Respect platform terms and third-party rights when using data outside ProspectPulse.
  • Do not attempt to bypass plan limits, credit checks, authentication, or security controls.

Availability

ProspectPulse may be unavailable during maintenance, incidents, data provider outages, Stripe issues, Supabase issues, workflow errors, or network failures. We may modify or discontinue features as the product evolves.

Limitation Of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, ProspectPulse is provided as-is and liability is limited to the amount paid for the service during the relevant billing period. This limitation should be reviewed by counsel before production use.
